OpenCV Inpainting Guidelines

🔤

Perfect for Text & Stamps

Ideal for deleting watermarks, text captions, signatures, and date stamps. The thin line structure is easily rebuilt by neighboring pixels.

🔍

Keep the Brush Tight

Adjust the brush size so it closely matches the target. Painting too far outside the object can introduce smudges and unwanted background blend.

⚠️

Small Objects Only

Because OpenCV uses mathematical interpolation (Telea's algorithm) without neural hallucination, it struggles with large objects. Focus on details, wires, and blemishes.
